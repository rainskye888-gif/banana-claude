Our Story

Born from a
Singular Obsession

In 1987, master woodworker Henri Dumont and Swiss horologist Marie Leclerc shared a single conviction: the world's most cherished objects were being surrendered to machines. Aurea Atelier was their quiet refusal.

From a small workshop in Lyon, their philosophy spread through private collectors' circles across Europe, Japan, and the Americas. Today, twelve master craftsmen continue the tradition — each piece requiring a minimum of 240 hours to complete by hand.

We accept no compromises. We use no substitutes. Every grain of walnut, every tooth of every gear, every curl of gilded acanthus is considered, weighed, and placed by human hands alone.

Questions & Answers

Everything You
Need to Know

Each Aurea music box is handcrafted entirely to order. The Classique and Reverie editions require 8–12 weeks from the moment your reservation is confirmed. Bespoke commissions begin with a private consultation and typically take 14–18 weeks depending on the complexity of your brief. We will communicate every milestone to you directly.
The Classique plays Swan Lake (Tchaikovsky). The Reverie adds Clair de Lune (Debussy) as a second melody. For the Bespoke commission, we work with our in-house arranger to transcribe any melody you wish — from a lullaby you remember to a contemporary composition. Custom melody arrangement is included in the Bespoke price.
The Classique includes standard engraving up to 20 characters on the wind-key plaque. The Reverie extends this to 40 characters with a choice of three calligraphic fonts. Bespoke commissions include unlimited engraving across multiple surfaces — the lid interior, the base, and the mechanism plate. All engravings are hand-cut by our master engraver.
We ship to 28 countries worldwide. The Reverie and Bespoke editions include white glove delivery — a dedicated courier will bring your piece in its presentation case, oversee unpacking, and demonstrate the winding mechanism in person. For the Classique, we use specialist fine-art shipping partners with full insurance and climate-controlled transit.
All Aurea pieces include a 5-year warranty covering mechanism and finish. We recommend light waxing of the walnut exterior once per year using our included care kit. The mechanism requires no lubrication for the first ten years under normal use. Should any service be required, we offer a complimentary collection and return service during the warranty period. Bespoke editions include lifetime maintenance.
Private atelier visits are available exclusively for Bespoke commission clients. During your visit, you will meet the master craftsman assigned to your piece, review material samples, choose your wood from our aged stock, and discuss the full scope of your commission. Visits are by appointment only and typically last a full afternoon. Travel arrangements can be assisted by our client concierge team.
